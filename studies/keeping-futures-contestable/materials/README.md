# Human-study materials packet

These are editable templates, not approved participant documents. An authorised researcher must replace every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}`, align the wording with the institutional decision, assign version numbers, and archive the approved copies under `data/frozen/governance/` before any human session.

## Packet contents

- `ETHICS_APPLICATION_TEMPLATE.md`: protocol-facing study summary and risk/data sections.
- `PARTICIPANT_INFORMATION_SHEET_TEMPLATE.md`: participant-facing information.
- `CONSENT_FORM_TEMPLATE.md`: consent statements, including separate artefact/quotation reuse choices.
- `DEBRIEF_TEMPLATE.md`: post-task explanation without claiming a result.
- `RECRUITMENT_NOTICE_TEMPLATE.md`: neutral recruitment copy.
- `DATA_MANAGEMENT_PLAN_TEMPLATE.md`: pseudonymisation, storage, access, withdrawal, deletion, and incident handling.

The templates deliberately keep directional hypotheses out of recruitment and pre-task wording. They disclose that the editing interface uses fixed pre-generated candidates and no remote AI call. They do not describe the software check as ethics approval.

## Required approved filenames

Copy the institutionally approved, placeholder-free versions to:


Keep contact lists and signed consent records outside the research-response corpus. Link them only through the approved pseudonymous-ID process.
